Alexandrie is an **open-source**, **self-hosted** knowledge management application. It combines Markdown writing, a document tree, search, controlled sharing, and file storage in one interface.

It works equally well as a personal notebook or a team wiki. Its purpose is simple: keep your knowledge in a space you control without giving up a modern writing experience.

## Why Alexandrie?

- **Sovereignty** — the instance, database, and files remain under your control.
- **Extended Markdown** — use standard Markdown alongside Mermaid diagrams, KaTeX formulas, callouts, colours, and more.
- **Clear organisation** — structure content with teams, workspaces, categories, and nested documents.
- **Controlled collaboration** — grant read, write, admin, or owner access where it is needed.
- **Available everywhere** — the application is an installable PWA with offline support and synchronisation.

## Who is it for?

Alexandrie is suitable for product and engineering teams, associations, research groups, small businesses, and individuals who want a self-hosted alternative or complement to Notion, Confluence, or Obsidian.

## Architecture at a glance

| Service | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Frontend | The Nuxt web interface used in the browser. |
| Backend | The Go API for authentication, permissions, documents, and backups. |
| MySQL | Application data and metadata. |
| RustFS | S3-compatible object storage for media and exports. |
