After signing up, create a team or workspace for your use case. Add a category for a domain — such as “Product”, “Infrastructure”, or “Personal” — then create your first document.

## A simple structure that scales

Start with few levels: one category per important subject, a welcome document in each, then child documents as needed. An overly deep hierarchy is often less useful than clear headings and good use of search.

## Invite collaborators

From team or workspace settings, invite the relevant people and choose their access level. Reserve admin and owner roles for people who need to manage members or structure.

## Write your first document

Type normally or use Markdown. Start with headings, lists, and links; consult the [Markdown guide](/markdown/bases) when you want to go further.
